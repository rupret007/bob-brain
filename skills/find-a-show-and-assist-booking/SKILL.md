# Find a show and assist booking

Use when Jeff wants help finding a gig, acting as band-manager assist, or supporting the human booker (for Rad Dad: Travis) without replacing them.

## Role split
- **Bob:** research rooms/festivals, rank by bookability, draft one-sheets and pitch text, package holds/run-of-show, watch application windows, draft texts to the band and to the booker.
- **Human booker:** owns relationships and makes contact. For Rad Dad that is Travis. Never auto-pitch a venue, booker, or festival under Jeff’s or Travis’s name.
- **Jeff:** yes before any outreach, pitch, book, or band-wide “we’re going for this room” send.

## Find the next show
1. Confirm which band and the target window (city/region, after which date, original vs cover, free vs paid, festival vs room).
2. Research real rooms and festivals that book that kind of act. Prefer venues with a clear booking path (email, form, booker name, open call dates).
3. Rank a short list by bookability (path clarity, fit, timing), not by vanity. Skip fake or guessed contacts.
4. Deliver a package in Bob chat: venue, why it fits, booking path, any deadline, what Travis/Jeff still must decide. No outreach yet.
5. If StoryBoard or another band OS is available, stage prospects there for the human booker; do not invent CRM rows as “booked.”

## One-sheet and pitch assist
1. Draft a fill-in-not-faked one-sheet (bio short, draw, links, EPKs, recent rooms). Mark unknowns as blanks.
2. Draft pitch email/DM in the booker’s voice only when asked. Show full recipient + body in Bob chat.
3. Send or hand off only after Jeff (and Travis when Rad Dad) explicitly yes. Prefer Travis sends; Bob may draft for him to paste, or send via Andrea/email only with that yes.

## Hold → confirmed → day-of
1. When a hold or confirm lands, package date, time, load-in, guarantee/door, and band group text.
2. Attach or link the stage phone lyrics page (inline expand + wake lock when MDM auto-lock is an issue).
3. Draft→yes→Andrea for band texts. Calendar invite only if Jeff asks.

## Festival application windows
1. Watch known windows (routine or manual). When a window opens, ping Jeff with the form link and what’s needed; do not submit without yes.
2. Prefill answers from known band facts; leave blanks rather than inventing credits or draws.

## Never
- Cold-email venues “as Travis” or “as the band manager” without an explicit send yes for that message.
- Replace the booker’s relationships or claim a show is booked before they confirm.
- Post social/SEO as a substitute for booking.
