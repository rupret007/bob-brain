## Proposals
### Codex
**Recommendation:** self-host Pipecat SmallWebRTC on the Mac mini: iPhone Safari ↔ WebRTC/Opus ↔ Silero VAD → sherpa-onnx streaming Zipformer STT → small local MLX language model → Kokoro TTS. Assume Apple Silicon with ≥16GB RAM; hardware is unverified. No Grok or paid inference.

Evidence: Pipecat recommends [SmallWebRTC for self-hosting](https://docs.pipecat.ai/client/concepts/choosing-a-transport); sherpa documents [streaming recognition on macOS](https://k2-fsa.github.io/sherpa/onnx/index.html); MLX Audio’s [`README.md`](https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio/blob/main/README.md) documents Kokoro.

**Top three fixes, ranked and sequenced** — filenames below are proposed:

1. **Establish reliable Safari audio and networking — `voice-client.ts`, `voice_server.py`.** One waveform-button tap requests microphone access, unlocks playback, and starts continuous duplex audio with echo cancellation. Serve page/signaling through private Tailscale HTTPS. WebRTC media needs a separately reachable ICE/UDP path over Tailscale; HTTPS proxying alone is insufficient. Validate that path on cellular before adding models. Sources: [Tailscale Serve](https://tailscale.com/docs/features/tailscale-serve), [WebKit WebRTC](https://webkit.org/blog/7763/a-closer-look-into-webrtc/). Target foreground Safari; background/locked-screen continuity needs device testing.

2. **Stream every stage — `voice_pipeline.py`.** Keep models loaded; decode microphone frames continuously and expose partial transcripts. Start with 300ms endpoint silence, a quantized 3B local instruction model, short context, and short spoken responses. Feed the first short clause into Kokoro immediately, then subsequent clauses; this is incremental phrase synthesis. Proposed end-of-user-speech → audible-response budget: endpoint 300ms + STT finalization 100ms + first LLM clause 350ms + TTS 300ms + transport/playback 150ms = **1.2s**. This is a target, not a measured guarantee.

3. **Make interruption cancellation complete — `voice_session.py`, `voice_latency.md`.** Keep listening during playback. Confirmed speech cancels LLM/TTS, clears outbound audio, and invalidates stale chunks using turn IDs. Retain only delivered assistant speech in history. Test 100 turns across Wi-Fi/cellular, speakerphone/AirPods, and interruptions; require p95 first audio <1.5s and interruption stop <200ms.
