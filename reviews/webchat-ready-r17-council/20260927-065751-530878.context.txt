**Borrow**

**Reject**

- Mid-reply deploy restarts (03:40 — one graceful restart end of round).

**Why (lowest R16 → R17 targets)**
- Self-improve 9.44 → surface install CTA in Settings without opening details.
- Voice 9.64 → transcript contrast + Grok-style call pill.
- Mobile / iPhone 9.88 → tab label truncation, chat ≥75% height (Playwright), bubble contrast.
- Music 9.74 → $0 **turnaround** + **cadence** coach + leading-tone (vii°) on progression scan.
