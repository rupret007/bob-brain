# Web Bob music handoff

Songwriting help remains available in chat. Paste lyrics, chords, or arrangement notes and ask for a specific revision.

The Songs tab, quick chip, notebook editor, recording upload and demo controls have been removed from the phone shell. Previously saved projects under `conductor/webchat/song-projects/` and their authenticated API remain intact; saved projects can still enter the provider reference context. Removing the interface does not delete or migrate those files.

Chat attachments retain their existing supported image, video and document formats; do not promise a replacement audio upload or demo interface. No music service signup, credential change or paid generation is part of this change. Logic Pro changes still require explicit confirmation.
